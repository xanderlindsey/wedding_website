How We Met

Some people find each other on the first try. Ella and Xander were not those people. They missed each other at church, shared a row in an 800-person Accounting class and never exchanged a word, and still somehow needed a dating app, a string of voice memos, and one very persuasive cartoon rat to finally get it right. Worth every almost.
