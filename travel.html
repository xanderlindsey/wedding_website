Getting Around

Shuttle

A shuttle will run continuously between the Livery Shopping Center in Danville and the reception venue throughout the evening.

Please plan to park at the Livery and take the shuttle — it's close, easy, and we want you to celebrate freely!

Driving

By Car

Danville is a beautiful 45-minute drive from Oakland via I-580 E and I-680 S.

Parking is available at the Livery Shopping Center — the shuttle will bring you directly to the reception.
